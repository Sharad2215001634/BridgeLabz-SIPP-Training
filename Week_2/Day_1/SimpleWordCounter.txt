Class WordCounter:
    Method countWords(paragraph):
        Convert paragraph to lowercase
        Remove punctuation
        Split by spaces into words
        For each word:
            If word in map
                map[word]++
            Else
                map[word] = 1
        Sort map by frequency
        Print word and count

Main Program:
    paragraph = "The news today is about the rise in technology trends."
    Call WordCounter.countWords(paragraph)
